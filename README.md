## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

• Provides logistics support.

• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.

• Lower prices for consumers.

• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

   We propose developing a Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), retailers, wholesalers, and consumers through a single online platform.

   Farmers can register and upload their available agricultural products, including quantity, quality, and price. Consumers and bulk buyers can directly view the products and place orders without depending on multiple intermediaries.

   The platform will also provide AI-based demand forecasting to predict the demand for agricultural products. A logistics management system will help arrange transportation and identify suitable delivery routes.

2. How It Addresses the Problem

   •Eliminates unnecessary intermediaries between farmers and consumers.

   •Allows farmers to receive better prices for their products.

   •Helps consumers purchase agricultural products at reasonable prices.

   •Provides direct communication between farmers and buyers.

   •Reduces transportation delays and supply chain inefficiencies.

   •Helps farmers understand market demand through AI predictions.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

   •AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Predicts future demand for agricultural products.

   •Direct Digital Marketplace: Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

   •Smart Logistics: Suggests efficient transportation routes.

   •Price Transparency: Displays product prices clearly to both farmers and buyers.

   •Real-Time Order Tracking: Allows users to monitor their orders.

   •Multilingual Support: Provides regional language support to help farmers use the platform easily.



## Technical Approach

### 1. Technologies to Be Used

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript – To create the website and user interface.

  • Python – For backend development and processing.

  • Django – To develop the web application.

  • MySQL – To store farmer, product and customer details.

  • Machine Learning – To predict the demand for agricultural products.

  • Google Maps API – To find the best delivery routes.

### 2. Methodology and Process for Implementation

  1.Farmers and customers register and log in to the platform.

  2.Farmers upload their products, available quantities and prices.

  3.Customers and bulk buyers view the available products.

  4.Customers select products and place orders.

  5.The system confirms the orders.

  6.The logistics system manages transportation and delivery.

  7.AI helps to find the best delivery routes.

  8.Products are delivered directly to customers.

  9.AI analyzes previous sales data and predicts future product demand.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

   The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using existing web technologies, databases, AI algorithms, and mapping services.

   The platform can initially be implemented in a small number of villages and gradually expanded to different regions.

   Farmers can access the platform using smartphones, making it convenient and accessible.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

   •Lack of digital awareness among some farmers.

   •Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.

   •Difficulty in maintaining product quality.

   •Transportation and delivery management challenges.

   •Changes in agricultural product prices.

   •Building trust between farmers and buyers.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

   •Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

   •Introduce regional language support.

   •Provide training and awareness programs for farmers.

   •Partner with local transportation providers.

   •Implement product quality verification.

   •Maintain transparent pricing and secure transactions.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience

   The platform primarily benefits farmers, consumers, FPOs, retailers, wholesalers, and agricultural businesses.

   It creates a direct connection between agricultural producers and buyers, improving accessibility and transparency in the agricultural market.

2. Benefits of the Solution

    Economic Benefits:

      •Increased earnings for farmers.

      •Reduced prices for consumers.

      •Reduced dependency on intermediaries.

    Social Benefits:
